\section{Contributions}
\label{sec:contributions}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Sarvesh Srinath}: repository setup, working conventions,
pinned dependencies, continuous integration and the pipeline that reruns
every notebook; the engine-level split and evaluation metrics; the final
test-set evaluation, error analysis and comparison with published results;
reproducibility checks; review and merging of pull requests. Phase 2: the
sensor windows, padding and shared training loop with nested early stopping;
the final deep learning test evaluation over five seeds, the bootstrap
comparisons and the README. Wrote Sections~\ref{sec:evaluation},
\ref{sec:results}, \ref{sec:dl-setup} and~\ref{sec:dl-results}.
\item \textbf{Trishit Swarnakar}: loading of FD001 and construction of the
RUL target; exploratory data analysis, sensor selection and the choice of
target cap; deep learning dataset coverage and padding analysis; single-factor
ablation studies across sequence lengths, regularisation, inputs, and hybrid
branches. Wrote Section~\ref{sec:data} and Section~\ref{sec:ablations}.
\item \textbf{Aryan Kumar}: degradation feature engineering (regime
normalisation, rolling statistics, rolling slopes and health index) and the
feature ablation. Phase 2: the two-branch and residual hybrid models.
Wrote Sections~\ref{sec:features} and~\ref{sec:hybrid}.
\item \textbf{Husain Khorakiwala}: model implementation, baselines and
hyperparameter tuning with engine-grouped cross validation; the RUL cap
experiment; feature importance and SHAP analysis; review of the test-set
evaluation and reproducibility work. Phase 2: the LSTM, temporal CNN and
Transformer, their tuning and learning curves. Wrote
Sections~\ref{sec:models} and~\ref{sec:dl-models}.
\item \textbf{Navodit Sharma}: literature review; assembly of the report;
presentation slides. Phase 2: the deep learning literature review and the
table of published FD001 deep results; review of the hybrid, test result and
ablation pull requests. Wrote the abstract and Sections~\ref{sec:intro},
\ref{sec:related}, \ref{sec:dl-related}, \ref{sec:discussion}
and~\ref{sec:conclusion}.
\end{itemize}
